## Privacy

This page explains what Dadbot does and does not do with information when you visit.

### Hosting

Dadbot.blog is served by GitHub Pages. When your browser requests a page, GitHub records the request in its own logs (including your IP address) for security purposes. We cannot switch those logs off, do not use them to identify visitors, and do not receive a copy.

### No analytics, no advertising, no profiling

Dadbot runs no analytics or usage statistics, no advertising, no affiliate links, no sponsored posts, no cross-site tracking and no ad profiling. The only way the site earns anything is the optional Buy Me a Coffee button, which leads to buymeacoffee.com and is governed by that service's own privacy policy. If you never click it, Dadbot does not contact it.

### Local storage on your device

Three features remember small settings in your browser's local storage. Nothing here is sent to Dadbot or anyone else; it stays on your device until you clear it.

- **Weather location and forecast** (`dadbot_weather_location_v2`, `dadbot_weather_forecast_v2`): remembers a location you searched for so the weather ticker works. Entries you choose yourself stay until you clear them; automatic ones expire after 24 hours.
- **Radio station lists** (`dadbot_radio_countries_v1`, `dadbot_radio_languages_v1`): caches the station directory for a day so the radio desk loads faster.
- **Hot Takes votes** (`dadbot-hot-takes-votes`): remembers your votes on the Hot Takes desk. Votes are stored on your device only, never sent anywhere.

### Services your browser contacts directly

The site itself is static, but three features make requests from your browser to other services:

- **Weather** ([api.open-meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/), [geocoding-api.open-meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/)): when you use the weather ticker, your browser asks Open-Meteo for forecasts and searches place names you type. Open-Meteo is an open-source service that needs no account.
- **Location lookup** ([ipapi.co](https://ipapi.co/)): on your first visit the weather ticker may ask ipapi.co to estimate your city from your IP address, purely to show local weather. You can instead type a location; your typed choice is what gets remembered. Dadbot does not receive the result.
- **Radio directory** ([de1.api.radio-browser.info](https://www.radio-browser.info/)): the radio desk fetches its station list from the community-run radio-browser.info directory. Playing a station connects your browser directly to that station's own stream server, which is outside our control.

### What Dadbot does not do

- No cookies are set by Dadbot.
- No accounts and no contact forms.
- No analytics or usage statistics — we genuinely do not know how many people read the site.
- No sale, rental or sharing of any visitor information, because none is collected by the site.
