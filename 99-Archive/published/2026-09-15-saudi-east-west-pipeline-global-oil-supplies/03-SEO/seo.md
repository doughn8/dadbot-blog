### Direct-answer blocks to preserve in the draft

- **Has four per cent of global oil supply already disappeared?** No. The figure is a conditional risk estimate if Saudi exports cannot be maintained; current reporting describes the pipeline as offline and recent rerouted flow as roughly four million barrels per day.[8]
- **Is seven million barrels per day the amount of oil now lost?** No. Seven million barrels per day is the reported full pipeline capacity; it is not the same as recent throughput or confirmed lost supply.[10]
- **How long can Saudi Arabia keep exporting?** Current estimates put the Yanbu cushion at roughly five to seven days, but that is an industry estimate and other storage or partial-flow arrangements may change the clock.[13][14]
- **How long could repairs take?** AP-cited regional officials said three to five weeks and allowed for partial operation during repairs; other reported estimates reach six weeks, so the draft must show the range rather than declare one timetable.[10][11]
- **Will petrol prices rise immediately?** Do not give a yes/no price guarantee. Explain that crude, refined products, freight, taxes and retail timing are separate questions, then refresh UK and European price data before drafting.
